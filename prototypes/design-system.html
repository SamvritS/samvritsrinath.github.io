The page reads as one continuous voice. The same face carries the headline, the interface, and the argument. This is the tighter system: one family, fewer files, and a body that matches the geometry of the headings.

The measure and leading do most of the work, so the page still holds at 18 pixels and 68 characters without a second face. The owner chose this single-family reading voice after the two-option review.

Rules

Balance headings, pretty paragraphs, tabular numerals, 60 to 70 characters for reading prose. Outfit carries the reading role. No mixed-family emphasis inside a headline.

05Figures

Three aspect modes, no forced crop.

The measured image set runs from 0.338 portrait to 7.150 panoramic. Charts use free and contain. Covers use the 16:10 frame with coverFit. The plate is light in both themes; the plot is not inverted.

06Atmosphere

Ultramarine, contour, and grain.

The atmosphere sits behind content, never behind reading prose. One layer animates. The contour is 2 layers of 5 paths. The grain is inline feTurbulence. Everything is static under reduced motion.
